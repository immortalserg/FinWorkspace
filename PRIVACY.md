# FinWorkspace Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

FinWorkspace is an open-source, self-hosted personal finance application. This policy describes the application's intended data handling. Each person or organization operating a FinWorkspace server is responsible for the privacy practices of that deployment.

## Data processed

Depending on the features you use, FinWorkspace may process account profiles, email addresses, financial accounts and balances, transaction histories, budgets, documents, and settings. Bank connections may involve account identifiers, balances, transactions, consent status and access credentials or tokens.

## Storage and control

FinWorkspace is designed to store application data on the server chosen by the person operating it. The server operator controls hosting, backups, access, retention and deletion. Installing FinWorkspace does not automatically send all financial data to its open-source maintainer.

## Bank connections

If you enable bank synchronization, FinWorkspace uses Enable Banking to connect to supported financial institutions with your authorization. Banking data is accessed subject to the permissions and consent you grant. FinWorkspace is intended to access banking information, not initiate payments. Enable Banking and your bank may process information under their own privacy notices.

## Optional integrations

Optional features, including AI providers, email notifications, investment providers and document-management integrations, may transfer data to the services you configure. Review their policies before enabling them. The precise data transferred depends on the integration and its configuration.

## Security and retention

The operator is responsible for securing the server, managing user access, backups, encryption where applicable, and appropriate retention and deletion practices. No software or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

## Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal data. Contact the administrator of the FinWorkspace instance you use to exercise these rights. For a self-hosted instance, that administrator is normally the relevant point of contact.

## Controller and contact

The identity and contact details of the data controller depend on the deployment. Consult the public Privacy and Terms pages of your particular FinWorkspace instance or contact its administrator. This repository-level notice does not identify a controller for every installation.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the software changes. The revision date above indicates the latest version of this repository-level document.

## Additional information

See [Security and privacy](docs/security.md) and the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md). This document is a general project policy and should be adapted by any operator offering FinWorkspace as a hosted service.
